Omara is a small group of people who like building things and writing about them.

This site is where we keep notes on projects, tools, and ideas worth sharing. Posts are published whenever there is something to say, with no fixed schedule.

## Get in touch

The code behind this site lives on [GitHub](https://github.com/Omara-org/omara-org.github.io). Open an issue there if you spot a mistake or want to start a conversation.
